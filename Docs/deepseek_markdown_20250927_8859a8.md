# Vending Machine Office Hub 🏢⚡

*A comprehensive headquarters for scaling your vending machine empire across Kenya and beyond.*

## 🌟 Project Overview

This facility serves as the central nervous system for a rapidly expanding vending machine network. Designed with scalability, efficiency, and employee wellness in mind, it combines operational excellence with innovative technology.

## 🎯 Vision Statement

> "To create the most efficient, tech-driven vending machine infrastructure in East Africa, supporting thousands of smart vending points while fostering innovation and employee satisfaction."

## 🏗️ Facility Zones & Layout

### Zone A: Reception & Client Interaction
- **Grand Lobby & Reception**: Branded welcome area
- **Client Showroom**: Live demonstrations of vending solutions
- **Meeting Rooms**: Investor and partner presentations
- **Visitor Washrooms**

### Zone B: Employee Wellness & Amenities
- **Locker Rooms**: Secure personal storage
- **Changing Rooms & Showers**: For logistics and production staff
- **Canteen/Cafeteria**: 150-seat capacity with product testing area
- **Employee Washrooms**

### Zone C: Logistics & Distribution
- **Loading/Unloading Bays**: 4 truck capacity
- **Restocking Prep Area**: Automated tray preparation
- **Delivery Vehicle Parking**: Dedicated logistics fleet

### Zone D: Office & Administration
- **Open-Plan Workstations**: 50+ employee capacity
- **Private Offices**: Management and HR
- **IT/Server Room**: Network operations center

### Zone E: Technology & Innovation Hub
- **IoT Monitoring Center**: Real-time vending machine analytics
- **R&D Laboratory**: New product development
- **Robotics/Automation Lab**: Restocking automation testing

### Zone F: Storage & Production
- **Dry Storage**: Smart shelving with RFID tracking
- **Cold Storage**: Refrigerated and frozen sections
- **Mini-Production Area**: Snack and beverage packaging
- **Maintenance Workshop**: Machine repair and upkeep

### External Facilities
- **Parking Lot**: 100+ vehicle capacity with EV charging
- **Accessible Pathways**: ADA-compliant design
- **Green Spaces**: Employee relaxation areas
