# 🇰🇪 Kenyan Business Landscape

This document provides an overview of **businesses in Kenya**, from micro-enterprises to large corporations. It can serve as a reference for marketplace platforms like **CEMall**.

---

## 1. Micro and Small Businesses (SMEs / Informal Sector)
Owner-managed, local, and low-capital businesses.

**Examples:**
- **Food & Beverage**
  - Street food vendors, kiosks, cafes, juice bars
  - Small bakeries, nyama choma joints
- **Retail**
  - Small shops (dukas), mobile phone stalls, clothing kiosks
  - Hawkers selling household items or groceries
- **Services**
  - Barber shops, salons, tailoring, cobblers
  - Motorbike (boda-boda) or car repair services
- **Agriculture**
  - Small-scale farms (vegetables, fruits, poultry, eggs)
  - Bee-keeping and dairy farming
- **Tech / Freelance**
  - Mobile phone repair, printing, graphic design
  - Freelance coding or online tutoring

---

## 2. Medium Enterprises
Formal businesses with 10–100+ employees.

**Examples:**
- **Retail & Distribution**
  - Mini-supermarkets (Chandarana, Quickmart franchises)
  - Pharmacies and boutique shops
- **Food Processing**
  - Packaged snacks, juice bottling, dairy processing
- **Transport & Logistics**
  - Small logistics firms, courier services, taxi companies
- **Tourism & Hospitality**
  - Small lodges, tour operators, travel agencies
- **Manufacturing**
  - Local furniture makers, garment factories, metalworks
- **Tech Startups**
  - Payment platforms, app development, e-commerce ventures

---

## 3. Large Businesses / Corporations
Formal, often multinational or nationally recognized companies.

**Examples:**
- **Agriculture & Food**
  - Kakuzi Ltd, Brookside Dairy, Del Monte Kenya
- **Banking & Finance**
  - KCB, Equity Bank, Co-operative Bank, Stanbic
- **Telecom & Tech**
  - Safaricom, Airtel Kenya, Liquid Telecom
- **Retail & Supermarkets**
  - Carrefour Kenya, Naivas
- **Manufacturing**
  - Bidco Africa (oils & soaps), East African Breweries Ltd
- **Energy & Infrastructure**
  - KenGen, KenolKobil, Total Kenya
- **Transport & Logistics**
  - Kenya Airways, Express Kenya, Cold Storage Nairobi

---

## 4. Emerging & Niche Sectors
High-growth sectors offering new opportunities.

- **E-commerce**
  - Jumia, Kilimall, Copia  
- **Renewable Energy**
  - Solar panel installers, biogas suppliers  
- **Health & Fitness**
  - Gyms, wellness centers, fitness apps  
- **Education & Online Learning**
  - E-learning platforms, coding bootcamps  
- **Creative & Digital**
  - YouTube creators, TikTok content creators, animation studios  

---

## 💡 Key Insights for Marketplace Platforms (CEMall)
- Focus on **SMEs and medium-sized businesses** needing online presence.  
- Food, retail, and small manufacturing are high-demand categories.  
- Large corporates could be targeted for **B2B integrations**.  
- Kenya’s informal economy is vast — dukas, small farmers, and artisans are key markets.
