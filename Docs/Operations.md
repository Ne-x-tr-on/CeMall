# 🛒 Multi-Vendor Marketplace Core Features

This document outlines the **core features** common to modern multi-vendor e-commerce platforms such as **CEMall, CS-Cart, Yo!Kart, Sharetribe, and BigCommerce**. It serves as a reference for building or understanding multi-vendor marketplaces.

---

## 1. Vendor Management
- Vendor registration and onboarding  
- Storefront creation (logo, description, policies)  
- Vendor dashboard to manage products, inventory, and orders  
- Performance tracking (sales, ratings, and reviews)  

---

## 2. Product Management
- Product CRUD operations (Create, Read, Update, Delete)  
- Support for product variations (size, color, model)  
- Stock/inventory management  
- Product categorization and tagging  

---

## 3. Order Management
- Order placement and processing  
- Status tracking: Pending → Processing → Shipped → Delivered  
- Refunds and cancellations  
- Vendor-specific order views  

---

## 4. Customer Management
- User registration/login  
- Customer dashboard (order history, saved items)  
- Wishlist and cart management  
- Review and rating system for products/vendors  

---

## 5. Payment & Commission
- Multiple payment gateway integrations (Stripe, PayPal, etc.)  
- Automatic commission calculation for vendors  
- Vendor payouts and transaction history  
- Support for multiple currencies (optional)  

---

## 6. Search & Navigation
- Advanced search with filters and categories  
- Sorting options (price, popularity, rating)  
- Product recommendations (optional AI-driven)  

---

## 7. Admin Panel / Platform Management
- Dashboard with analytics for total sales, active users, and vendors  
- Vendor approval and moderation  
- Platform-wide settings (taxes, shipping, discounts)  
- Reporting tools for performance monitoring  

---

## 8. Communication & Notifications
- Email/SMS notifications for order updates  
- In-platform messaging between vendor and customer (optional)  
- Alerts for low stock or system events  

---

## 9. Security & Compliance
- Role-based access control (admin/vendor/customer)  
- Secure payment handling  
- GDPR or local data compliance (if applicable)  
- Fraud detection features  

---

## 10. Scalability & Extensibility
- Modular architecture to add features later (API or plugins)  
- Mobile-responsive or dedicated mobile apps  
- Multi-language and multi-region support (optional)  

---

## Summary
These features ensure that a **multi-vendor marketplace** can function **autonomously and scalably**, supporting multiple vendors and customers while providing a unified and secure platform.  

The **admin dashboard, vendor tools, and customer experience** are central to all these systems, making them efficient, scalable, and user-friendly.
